# A. Problem Statement and Motivation

Depression amongst university students is a relevant issue and can impact negatively on the academic performance and social interactions of the students. The identification of patterns related to depression can increase knowledge about the factors associated with the problems faced by students' mental health.

In this project, I intend to look at whether demographic, academic, family, social, and health-related patterns can be used in predicting the level of depression among university students using supervised classification algorithms. By employing a student records dataset, I intend to look into the patterns associated with depression and use supervised classification models to classify depressed students from the not depressed students.

The main purpose of this project is to investigate the application of data-driven methods in the research process related to mental health of students. These models are for research purposes only.
